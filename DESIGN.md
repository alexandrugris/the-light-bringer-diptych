# Visual direction

The visual reference is the Gemini visualization collected at the bottom of the main poem page and in [Alexandru Gris's publication](https://blog.alexandrugris.ro/2026/04/the-light-bringer-poetic-reflection-of.html). Its reading note links back to this gallery.

Its inviting, slightly childish hope makes the poem's implications more unsettling. The website borrows its illustrated science-fiction vocabulary:

- Tall arched panels with double outlines, like pages in a futuristic picture book.
- A dark celestial frame around pale blue skies, warm gold, soft botanical colors, and rose accents.
- Small planets, orbital lines, flowers, and stars that connect technological promise to an apparently gentle natural world.
- Light reading panels for the first poem and the annotations; a deeper cosmic blue for The Others.

The approved crayon illustrations remain the principal artwork. The orbit-and-flower ornaments are local SVGs, and the poem typography remains Source Serif 4 throughout.

The technologist illustration has a wider background so it fills the arched cover panel on desktop and mobile while preserving the full standing pose.

The poems and original reading-note bodies are preserved verbatim. The annotation interactions use the existing numbered verse markers, desktop connectors, and mobile expansions beneath each selected verse.
